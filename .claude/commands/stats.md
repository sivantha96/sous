---
description: Answer a question about what Sous has done. Usage: /stats <org> [question]
---

1. Run `./bin/sous stats <org>` (add an ISO date to change the 30-day window), `./bin/sous memory hits <org>` and, if the question is about cost, `./bin/sous tokens --org <org> --label stats`.
2. Answer $ARGUMENTS from those numbers only. Say which numbers you used, and say plainly when the log doesn't hold the answer.
